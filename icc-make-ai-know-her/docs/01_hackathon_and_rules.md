## Event
- **ICC Global Hackathon powered by Ignyte** (DIFC, Dubai). Theme: "Beyond Boundaries, Empowering Women, Inspiring Sport".
- Prize pool USD 10,000: overall USD 5,000; Student/Entrepreneur USD 2,500; All-Female Team USD 2,500.
- **Entry deadline: Mon 5 Oct 2026** (as reported from the event page; confirm exact time and timezone). Showcase at Dubai AI Festival 26-27 Oct 2026 (handoff) / demo date Mon 26 Oct (earlier brief); both reported. Organisers may change dates without notice (the showcase already slipped before).
- Participants: about 9,000 (reported by the team; recheck on the platform before using publicly).
- Winners must deliver a usable solution with documentation. Finalists keep their IP.
- ICC announced "pilot opportunities with ICC and its global partners".

## Rules (verbatim from the page text Sir Jabin pasted)
**Submission criteria**
- Open to students, startups, and scale-ups worldwide
- Cross-functional teams (tech + business + design) will be valued
- Team must consist of at least one female contributor
- Submission deadline, finalist announcement, and presentation dates are at the sole discretion of organizers, and can be changed without notice. All such changes will be updated on the hosting page

**Tracks**
- Idea track: Concept note, mockups, or wireframes
- **Prototype track: Working prototype, demo video (3 min max)**  <- our track
- Scale-up track: Functional product with proof of traction/pilot readiness

Women-integrated teams must have strong representation of women founders or developers.

**Minimum submission:** pitch deck / solution overview (max 5 slides or 2 pages: problem statement, proposed solution, impact for sports / women in sports), summary, link to a video (max 3 min for the prototype track).

**Selection criteria**
- Clear and well-defined USP
- Technical Innovation & Use of ICC
- Innovative features
- Sustainability and scalability of solution
- Technical capabilities
- Ease of implementation
- Problem/Solution fit

## Judging criteria and weights (build to these)
| # | Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Innovation & Creativity | 25% | Originality; creativity solving a real challenge in sports; differentiation from existing solutions |
| 2 | Technical Feasibility & Execution | 20% | Functionality and soundness of the prototype; scalability/adaptability for ICC and broader sports; quality of documentation, architecture, design |
| 3 | Impact on Women in Sport | 20% | Tangible benefits for women athletes, teams and fans; contribution to equity, visibility, empowerment; inclusivity and gender fairness in design |
| 4 | Value to Fans, Athletes & Sports Ecosystem | 15% | Relevance for fans, players, coaches, sponsors; enhances fan/athlete experience across ICC events; broader contribution to global sport innovation |
| 5 | Sustainability & Inclusivity | 10% | Environmental impact and sustainability; accessibility, affordability, **multi-language support**; representation of women and diversity in team and product design |
| 6 | Presentation & Storytelling | 10% | Clarity and persuasiveness; quality of demo; communicating vision, impact, alignment with ICC |

**Bonus recognition:** women-led teams; **cross-sport applicability (beyond cricket)**; **pilot-readiness for ICC and partners**.

## Official problem statements
(1) Sport Visibility & Engagement; (2) Next-Gen Fan Experiences; (3) Athlete Health, Performance & Inclusivity.
**Our choice: (1) Sport Visibility & Engagement**, at a new discovery interface: AI assistants. The team's rule: fan engagement only; athlete health is out (needs hardware or long-run proof).

## Already built by ICC / Google / others (do not headline these)
ICC Insights AI companion; Gemini "Explore Cricket" (Gemini is the ICC's Official AI Fan Companion for the Men's T20 World Cup 2026); ICC.tv in 9 languages; AI commentary (other teams); Freedom2hear abuse moderation; WSC Sports auto-highlights; FitrWoman. Our relationship to them: we are the layer that makes sure such AI surfaces don't default to men.
